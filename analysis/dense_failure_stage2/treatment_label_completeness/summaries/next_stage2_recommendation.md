# Next Stage-2 recommendation

Recommend exactly one separately authorized direction: **replace the old single clean KEEP/INTERVENE target with a prospectively specified set-valued treatment target built from replay-validated successful-action sets before any further Stage-2 fitting.**

## Why it matters

The expanded audit changed 99.2% of old KEEP labels and 54.2% of old INTERVENE labels to MIXED. Only four clean KEEP states remain, so neither another binary treatment head nor its planned five-fold evaluation is currently identifiable.

## Hypothesis tested by that future direction

A target that represents all observed successful first actions, including explicit mixed/ambiguous sets, may align supervision with what the executor/search actually establishes instead of forcing one action or a binary treatment-need label.

## Interpretation of a future result

- Positive: a prospectively frozen set-valued objective improves held-out valid-action recognition and sequential rescue/preservation, supporting target misspecification as a major Stage-2 bottleneck.
- Negative: failure despite adequate set-valued support would strengthen the case for representation or on-policy state-diversity limitations.

## What must not be overinterpreted

The current bounded sets are not exhaustive causal action labels, and search saturation at 200 does not prove completeness. A future set-valued fit would not by itself establish deployment benefit; it would still require held-out routed rollouts and preservation accounting.

This recommendation is a strategic target/objective change and is **not authorized by this audit**. No Stage-2 retraining, Stage-1 change, new search, or external evaluation has been started.
