# One unexecuted recommendation

**Close the tested local and H≤8 WRITE-routing hypothesis, and reframe the research question around unresolved nonlocal intervention structure.** This is a recommendation for the next research framing, not a claim that harm is intrinsically nonlocal or authorization to implement a new method.

The completed result is W-LOCAL-WEAK followed by qualified W-PROP-C / WRITE-S4. WRITE propagation produces statistically supported small gains, yet none meets the frozen materiality and useful-precision requirements. READ independently failed its local and short-horizon usefulness gates. Returning to the four-action classifier or deploying a WRITE critic is therefore unsupported by these measurements.

The key remaining question is whether information outside the tested local/short-horizon representations can support dependable intervention selection. No new experiment or strategic pivot is specified or executed here. Any future method, horizon extension, or joint trajectory study requires a separate user-authorized plan.

The required candidate comparison and independent review considered three alternatives:

| Candidate | Uncertainty reduced / decision enabled | Cost | Main weakness |
|---|---|---|---|
| Scoped closure and reframing (selected) | Records that the tested families are not useful; prevents unsupported critic deployment | No additional compute | Leaves longer horizons and richer information unresolved |
| Fixed H16 continuation (not selected) | Could test later emergence within its eligible subset | Additional paired rollout, fixed OOF fits and controls; not executed or timed | Only 1,507 states / 352 UIDs / 348 groups, with 43 harmful and 6 beneficial flips; sharply narrower source support |
| Bounded joint trajectory reasoning (not selected) | Could test interaction/history-dependent correction | New expensive, separately specified search and validation action | Local weakness does not establish that joint reasoning will help; prior READ search is stopped and incomplete |

The strongest objection to closure is that H8 gains are positive and may continue. That remains true: these curves do **not** establish saturation. However, the frozen +0.10 criterion is improvement over H0, not an absolute Spearman threshold; the observed +0.0561 gain has a 95% upper bound of 0.0852, and useful precision fails. No horizon beyond H8 is needed to substantiate this explicitly bounded result.

The reviewer initially ranked H16 first. One metadata-only eligibility audit found 1,217 GQA states, 278 historical TextVQA states, only 12 ChartQA states, and no canonical TextVQA at H16. This would answer a narrower question and cannot be extrapolated to the full population. After correcting the threshold interpretation and considering that support, the same read-only reviewer ranked scoped closure first with high confidence. No H16 rollout, target generation, or fitting occurred. See [review reconciliation](final_review_reconciliation.md) and [support audit](h16_support_only_audit.json).

Execution stops with this completed phase. Semantic/source/LODO refits, routed transfer, external deployment, WRITE search, and a joint router were gated off or outside scope. Phase85 search remains stopped.
